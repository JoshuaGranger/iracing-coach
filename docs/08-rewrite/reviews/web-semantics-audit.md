## The reported bug

The time-delta zero line on Traces is `referenceLapFor` (`web/src/investigation/geometry/trace-model.ts:372-382`): the fastest selected lap that passes `isCleanLap`, else the lowest-numbered selected lap. The web's `isCleanLap` (`:403-406`) adds `comparison_eligible !== false` to the old clean test, and `referenceLapFor` passes zero incident points (`:378`). So whenever none of the selected laps is backend-comparable (traffic, restart, off-track, damage), the zero line is simply the first lap by number, whatever its pace. The owner's laps 6 to 40 must all carry a backend exclusion, as every G2 lap does; lap 2 then wins because it has the lowest number.

The old app picked the fastest clean selected lap, where clean meant complete, green, no pit time and no incident points, with no comparability test (`UI/TelemetryWorkspace.razor:964-966`, `:1478-1484`); it fell back to the first selected lap only when no selected lap was clean. The backend's own reference lap (pit loss, pace attribution) is the fastest clean lap and is reported unavailable when there is none (`py/analysis_engine.py:8436-8446`); it never takes an arbitrary lap. The sign is right in all three: lap minus reference, positive is slower (`py/lap_reference.py:222`, `:341-372`).

On G2, with the web's code:

| Selection | Web zero line | Old rule | Deltas at the last bin |
|---|---|---|---|
| Default (Best three: 6, 3, 11) | lap 3, 28.417 s | lap 6, 28.408 s | lap 6 −0.017 s |
| Race › "Run 1: earlier to final green laps" › Inspect (laps 1–5, 36–40) | lap 1, 29.506 s (start lap, Excl) | lap 3 | every other lap −0.867 to −1.042 s |
| Laps 2, 6, 7, 8, 9, 40 (the owner's set) | lap 2, 28.503 s | lap 6 | 6 −0.100, 9 −0.075, 7 +0.017, 8 +0.058, 40 −0.000 |

The spotlight lap never sets the zero line, in either app; neither app names the zero lap (RA-049). The fix is T1 below; T2, T6 and T7 come from the same three-way split of what "clean" means.

## Per-view notes

### Traces

**Reference lap and sign.** Delta = this lap's time since its first bin minus the reference lap's time since its first bin, per bin (`trace-model.ts:121-149`); positive means behind. That matches the old app (`UI/TelemetryWorkspace.razor:2313-2331`) and the backend definition "comparison lap minus reference lap; positive is slower" (`py/lap_reference.py:222`). The reference choice is the defect (T1). Two smaller differences remain: incident laps can become the reference (incidents forced to 0, `trace-model.ts:378`), and each lap is anchored at its own first bin while the backend anchors both laps at the first bin both cover (T10; the old app shared the web's anchoring). The lap rail's `+0.xxx` deltas use yet another reference, the fastest comparison-eligible lap of the race (`trace-model.ts:62-66`), so the rail and the delta row can name different zero laps even after T1; both should come from one named rule.

**Spotlight versus reference.** The spotlight only changes stroke, opacity and draw order and puts that lap first in the cursor cards (`trace-model.ts:241-251`, `cursor/cursor-config.ts:151`); it never changes values or the zero line. Same as the old app (`UI/TelemetryWorkspace.razor:978-989`, `:1061-1070`). Neither app tells the driver which lap is zero; label it (RA-049).

**Comparison presets in the URL.** `comparison=` (and `section=`) only choose what Race, Runs and Sections show: the shell passes them through (`web/src/ui/investigation-slot.tsx:124-125`) and `selectedComparison` falls back to `default_comparison_id`, then the first comparison (`investigation-model.ts:419-421`), as the old app did (`UI/RaceInvestigationView.razor:168-171`). Traces ignores the comparison. Its laps come from one of four places: Best three when the view mounts (`TracesView.tsx:61`); the lead action's `lap_numbers` after Race › Inspect (`RaceView.tsx:253`, `InvestigationWorkspace.tsx:134-137`); the two passes of a section after Sections › Open in Traces (`SectionsView.tsx:299`); or the driver's clicks. The Inspect selection is not in the URL, so reloading `…/traces?comparison=run-1-finish` opens Best three. In G2 the two comparisons select laps 1–5 and 36–40 (`run-1-finish`) and 43–47 and 78–82 (`run-2-finish`, the default); every one is Excl. An Excl lap gets selected in three ways: an observed-finish comparison keeps excluded laps by design and Inspect copies its laps exactly (the old app did the same on purpose, `UI/TelemetryWorkspace.razor:754-759`); Best three falls back to complete green laps when no lap is comparable (G2 default 6, 3, 11, all Excl); or by hand.

**Which laps are comparable.** The web uses three definitions: `isComparable` (backend `comparison_eligible`, complete, no exclusion reasons: the Excl and Partial chips and the default pool), `isCleanLap` (green, no pit, no incidents and backend-eligible: the Clean filter and the zero line) and the fastest-lap test (backend-eligible only: magenta, rail deltas). The old app used one: complete, green, no pit time, no incident points (`UI/TelemetryWorkspace.razor:1478-1484`); the backend codes are `exclusion_reasons` (`openapi:1126-1143`). The documented decision of 0.14.1 is that the visual comparison does not inherit coaching exclusions such as traffic (`race-analysis.md:207`); the owner's expectation for the zero line is "the fastest comparable lap". Pick one definition and use it for the zero line, the Clean filter, the fastest lap and the defaults (T1, T2, T6, T7).

**Bin alignment.** The traces body has no lap-fraction channel (the 15 fixed signals, `crates/coach-views/src/traces.rs:36-164`), so bin i is drawn at (i + 0.5) / 160 (`trace-model.ts:55-61`), the contract rule (`openapi:241-250`) and the backend's bucket centre (`py/analysis_engine.py:2359`). Chart paths, cursor, map marker and readouts all use that grid. Per-bin values are the backend's bin representatives: speed and throttle medians, brake the bin peak (`brake_mean` is a separate key the catalog does not serve), steering the signed peak, session time the median (`py/analysis_engine.py:2346-2368`). The old app drew the same backend points, so there is no difference here. The Brake trace is a per-bin peak while the Sections brake is a bin mean from another producer; each is labelled for what it is.

**Readout formatting and units.** `formatSignalValue` (`cursor/cursor-core.ts:47-72`) matches the old cursor case by case (`UI/wwwroot/analysis-telemetry-cursor.js:58-83`): delta signed to 3 decimals in s, speed 1 decimal mph, pedals as whole percent, steering as Left/Right degrees with the basis' decimals, slip and yaw signed to 1 decimal, G to 2. Steering ids whose catalog unit is rad are converted to degrees (G2 `steering` rad, converted; `steering-wheel-angle` deg, as recorded). Cosmetic gaps are listed in T14.

**Track position average and cursor.** The readout under the map is the mean of every selected lap at the cursor bin for speed, throttle, brake and delta (`trace-model.ts:347-369`, `cursor/telemetry-cursor.ts:159-173`), the old scope (`UI/TelemetryWorkspace.razor:1689-1720`). Its delta is averaged against the same zero lap, so it inherits T1: in the owner's case it read negative. The map's metric colour is the median of the selected laps and the readout the mean, as in the old app. The cursor starts at 25 % in both; after Inspect the old app moved it to the middle of the inspected region (T12).
