3. **Major — SDK access failures become permission to upload on track.** [collector/src/sdk.rs:229](<C:/Users/joshu/source/repos/iRacing Coach/collector/src/sdk.rs:229>), [collector/src/win/map.rs:35](<C:/Users/joshu/source/repos/iRacing Coach/collector/src/win/map.rs:35>). 4. **Major — Folder scans continue after the gate closes.** [collector/src/uploader.rs:213](<C:/Users/joshu/source/repos/iRacing Coach/collector/src/uploader.rs:213>), [collector/src/scan.rs:124](<C:/Users/joshu/source/repos/iRacing Coach/collector/src/scan.rs:124>). Recursive setup traversal, metadata reads and pending-journal reconciliation can continue after the car goes on track. **Smallest fix:** pass the gate into scans and journal loading, check during traversal, and retain unfinished work when interrupted.

5. **Major — Reservations neither protect headroom nor accurately bound spool use.** [server/src/ingest.rs:338](<C:/Users/joshu/source/repos/iRacing Coach/server/src/ingest.rs:338>). Incoming bytes disappear from the accounting as `durable_offset` increases; free-space checks ignore other uploads’ outstanding reservations; different hashes can reserve concurrently. Conversely, a resume counts its existing reservation twice, so an upload accepted near the limit can become permanently unable to resume while the NAS is down. **Smallest fix:** atomically admit reservations, count existing incoming/ready/restaged bytes, subtract all outstanding reservations from available headroom, and add only an upload’s incremental reservation.

6. **Major — Concurrent creates can erase acknowledged chunks.** [server/src/ingest.rs:215](<C:/Users/joshu/source/repos/iRacing Coach/server/src/ingest.rs:215>), [ingest.rs:292](<C:/Users/joshu/source/repos/iRacing Coach/server/src/ingest.rs:292>). Two creates can both observe `Nothing` before either takes the hash lock. After the first creates an upload and receives chunks, the second takes the lock, deletes its `.part`, and resets the row to zero using its stale decision. **Smallest fix:** acquire the hash lock before reading upload/blob state and re-evaluate under that lock.

7. **Major — Idle cleanup can delete an upload that just became active.** [server/src/ingest.rs:374](<C:/Users/joshu/source/repos/iRacing Coach/server/src/ingest.rs:374>). Cleanup selects stale hashes before acquiring their locks. A chunk can update and acknowledge one of those uploads before cleanup obtains its lock; cleanup then deletes the newly active `.part` and row without checking `updated_at` again. **Smallest fix:** recheck state and cutoff under the hash lock before deleting either the file or row.

8. **Major — Restaging bypasses spool reservations and disk headroom.** [server/src/handlers.rs:120](<C:/Users/joshu/source/repos/iRacing Coach/server/src/handlers.rs:120>), [server/src/archiver.rs:489](<C:/Users/joshu/source/repos/iRacing Coach/server/src/archiver.rs:489>). Reanalysis copies archived recordings into NVMe without capacity admission. With uploads already reserving the remaining space, restaging can consume SQLite/WAL headroom or exceed the spool limit. **Smallest fix:** reserve restage bytes through the same admission mechanism before copying, releasing the reservation on failure or completion.

9. **Major — Restaged recordings acquire a fresh mtime and fail analysis.** [server/src/archiver.rs:486](<C:/Users/joshu/source/repos/iRacing Coach/server/src/archiver.rs:486>). 10. **Major — A torn journal tail destroys the next appended event.** [collector/src/journal.rs:175](<C:/Users/joshu/source/repos/iRacing Coach/collector/src/journal.rs:175>), [journal.rs:188](<C:/Users/joshu/source/repos/iRacing Coach/collector/src/journal.rs:188>). 12. **Major — Garage hashing can publish a hash for a changing setup file.** [collector/src/garage_io.rs:95](<C:/Users/joshu/source/repos/iRacing Coach/collector/src/garage_io.rs:95>), [garage_io.rs:128](<C:/Users/joshu/source/repos/iRacing Coach/collector/src/garage_io.rs:128>).
