# iRacing Coach companion app — review handoff

## Purpose

This document consolidates hands-on product feedback from the current application build. It is an intake/handoff, not an implementation specification. The screenshots cited throughout the review are listed at the end.

## Executive summary

The application contains promising functionality, especially Race Analysis, but repeatedly suffers from low information density, inconsistent layout decisions, weak interaction design, and severe performance problems. Several screens feel mechanically assembled from oversized cards instead of being designed around the user's actual decisions.

The highest-value product direction is:

1. Make Live Telemetry's richer **Driving traces** experience the primary, customizable dashboard rather than a secondary section.
2. Turn Race Analysis > **Technical Data** into a genuinely useful crew-chief/engineer experience: interpretation, prioritization, evidence, and concrete actions rather than isolated statistics.
3. Make Setups functional end to end, with clear availability indicators and no ambiguous dead ends.
4. Fix severe performance issues before polishing: some navigation takes about 20 seconds, and restoring from the system tray appears computationally expensive.
5. Establish compact, consistent layout primitives instead of oversized tiles and one-off controls.

## Cross-cutting issues

### Severe performance

- Navigating from **Progressive Tuning** to **Race Analysis** took approximately 20 seconds.
- Restoring/showing the app from the Windows system tray also appears computationally expensive.
- Normal navigation and window restoration should feel immediate. If expensive loading is unavoidable, it should be asynchronous and visibly communicated.
- Investigate unnecessary view reconstruction, synchronous backend calls, repeated analysis/data loading, and full-tree rerenders during navigation or window activation.

### Wasteful information density

- Many screens use very large cards to show one or two short values.
- Borders, backgrounds, and empty space often carry more visual weight than the data.
- Prefer compact rows, tables, grouped metrics, and layouts sized to their content.
- Large screens should not simply stretch compact widgets while leaving their content at the same tiny size.

### Consistency and shared components

- Repeated concepts should use the same components rather than visually similar one-off implementations.
- Example: Settings **Troubleshooting** still does not match **Connections**, despite multiple development passes. They should share the exact disclosure-bar component, including dimensions, spacing, typography, border, icon, and states.
- Comparable workflows should behave comparably, especially race selection/navigation in Race Analysis and Progressive Tuning.

### Text/glyph corruption

- A stray bracket-like or `{}`-like glyph appears after **Richmond** in multiple places, including Recent races and Race Planning selectors/summary text.
- Similar stray characters appear in prose, such as after `position(s)`.
- This resembles a failed icon, unsupported glyph, bad separator, or incorrectly rendered metadata marker.

### Trace quality and animation

- Some traces appear overly jagged.
- A noisy section of the live steering trace visibly fuzzes, jumps, and changes shape as it travels across the screen.
- Historical samples should remain visually stable once plotted. The time window should glide sideways smoothly as new samples enter.
- Axis scale, sample spacing, history window, geometry, and downsampling should not fluctuate from frame to frame.
- A legitimately noisy signal may look dense, but it must not shimmer or mutate due to repeated resampling/redrawing.

## Screen-by-screen critique

## Home

Overall, the Home screen is fair/acceptable.

Problems:

- **Best clean lap** and **pace trend** are not calculated or displayed for all races where they appear expected.
- Determine whether this is missing evidence, inconsistent eligibility logic, a calculation defect, or a display defect. The UI should distinguish those cases rather than silently leaving unexplained gaps.
- The final/rightmost column must show the same metric on every row. It should not change randomly between rows.
- The chosen rightmost metric should be carefully scrutinized, meaningful, and consistently available where evidence supports it—not filler.

## Live Telemetry

This screen has received the most detailed directional feedback.

### Primary product correction

The bottom **Driving traces / Track and telemetry** section is essentially the intended primary Live Telemetry experience. The current top grid is the wrong implementation of the primary function.

Desired direction:

- Promote the rich Driving traces experience into the primary customizable dashboard.
- Make its components available through the existing **Build/Customize** tools and grid-layout functionality.
- Allow the user to add, remove, resize, and arrange rich components such as stacked recent traces, current-lap position, current telemetry values, and related live metrics.
- Remove the separate secondary bottom **Driving traces** section. There should be one unified customizable live telemetry surface, not a simplistic primary dashboard plus a better secondary dashboard.

### Trace space usage

- In full-screen mode, Throttle, Brake, and Speed cards are extremely tall while the actual plot occupies only a thin horizontal band.
- There is excessive empty space above and below the traces.
- Either expand plots vertically to use their cards or collapse cards around the plots.
- The compact live popout is a much better proportional reference: its plots fill their cards and combine labels, values, gridlines, and traces coherently.
- Preserve that density and proportional behavior at larger sizes rather than stretching empty containers.

### Live trace stability

- Noisy steering history fuzzes heavily as it moves across the chart.
- The chart looks as though it is constantly being rebuilt, rescaled, or inconsistently resampled.
- The history should glide smoothly sideways; previously plotted signal shapes should remain fixed.
- Avoid redraw-induced shimmer, unstable interpolation, changing sample buckets, and frame-to-frame geometry changes.

### Popout window controls

- The top-right popout controls are deformed and do not read as standard Windows controls.
- Minimize appears as a bent-corner glyph; maximize/restore resembles a diagonal arrow.
- Sizing, stroke weight, alignment, highlighting, and hit areas are inconsistent.

### Invalid and truncated content

- `32767 laps remaining` is almost certainly an invalid/sentinel value leaking into the UI. It should be treated as unknown/unavailable, not displayed literally.
- Right-side content can truncate or wrap awkwardly, including the Coach Cue.

## Race Analysis

This is currently the most developed application function. The foundation is promising, but important quality issues remain.

### Laps & runs alignment

- The lap list is an alignment disaster: lap numbers, colored dots, time, delta, status markers, fuel, flags, and pit buttons do not occupy stable columns.
- Rows shift horizontally based on which badges or controls are present.
- Run headers/counts feel disconnected from the row grid.
- Define a consistent column structure so every value can be scanned vertically.
- The bottom action bar also feels unevenly spaced.

### Suspicious lap-time precision

- Displayed lap times appear quantized to 0.050-second increments (examples include `.200`, `.350`, `.400`, `.450`, `.600`, `.700`, and `.850`).
- This suggests premature rounding, low-resolution sampling, or a calculation/serialization issue.
- Verify the full timing pipeline rather than merely formatting extra decimal places.

### Jagged traces

- Some Race Analysis traces look excessively jagged. Determine whether this reflects raw data, unstable resampling, insufficient anti-aliasing, incorrect point density, or another rendering issue.
- Do not obscure meaningful telemetry with indiscriminate smoothing, but render it clearly and consistently.

### Technical Data — highest product opportunity

Technical Data is where the crew chief/engineer should come to life. Right now it is visually uninspiring and functionally too close to a sparse statistic dump.

Current problems:

- Huge tiles contain tiny amounts of text surrounded by empty space.
- The rigid quadrant layout wastes a widescreen viewport.
- Metrics are fragmented across oversized cards instead of organized into scannable relationships.
- Vertically centered labels/values increase eye travel.
- Section backgrounds and borders overpower the actual information.
- Very wide **What to carry forward** bars contain only one short sentence.
- The screen does not build an engineering narrative or help the user prioritize decisions.

Desired direction:

- Convert evidence into diagnosis, significance, confidence, and concrete next actions.
- Explain what matters most, why it matters, what evidence supports it, and what the driver should change or monitor.
- Group related metrics so relationships are visible rather than presenting isolated facts.
- Use compact tables/rows and purposeful visualizations where they improve comparison.
- Preserve backend evidence constraints: do not overclaim beyond supported evidence.

## Race Planning

The screen is basically acceptable, but not compelling or inspiring.

### Recommended Opening Plan layout

- The headline `Resolve an authoritative fuel decision before choosing a stop plan` does not fit its container.
- Its wrapped lines overlap due to insufficient line-height and/or an overly constrained text column.
- It competes horizontally with the race/range graphic.
- Use responsive type/line-height and allow a stacked layout when required.

### Bottom-half content is not useful

The criticism applies specifically to the visible **Race priorities** and **Tires and long-run approach** content below the separate Planning assumptions disclosure. It does **not** apply to the unseen/collapsed Planning assumptions content.

Problems:

- The guidance is generic, repetitive, and not meaningfully connected to session evidence or race decisions.
- `Protect the RF`, `repeatable entries`, `progressive brake release`, and `alter one input at a time` are broad coaching phrases without measurable targets, triggers, or tradeoffs.
- Start and Long run guidance substantially duplicate one another.
- Either replace this with evidence-backed, session-specific actions or remove it.

## Setups

The Setups screen currently appears effectively useless because its primary workflow cannot be completed.

- Dropdowns are extremely difficult or unreliable to operate.
- There is no clear indication of which vehicle/track combinations have usable data.
- The interface should visibly distinguish combinations that are available, unavailable, incomplete, or incompatible before the user commits to a selection.
- Avoid unexplained dead ends. State exactly what data is missing and what is required.
- Highest-priority acceptance criterion: a user can successfully create at least one setup end to end.

## Progressive Tuning

The workflow seems acceptable for now.

- Clicking the tab should initially show a race list comparable to Race Analysis.
- Race selection and navigation should be consistent between Race Analysis and Progressive Tuning.
- Users should not need to learn two different workflows for opening the same race/session context.
- Navigation from Progressive Tuning to Race Analysis exposed the approximately 20-second performance problem noted above.

## Settings

### Data section wastes space

- Two very large cards show only `Coach folder — App home` and `iRacing files — Found`.
- This belongs in compact labeled rows or a concise status area.
- The separate `Ready` badge is largely redundant with `Found`.
- The layout feels generated around containers rather than designed around the small amount and low complexity of the content.

### Troubleshooting/Connections mismatch

- The Troubleshooting bar is still not identical to the Connections bar despite repeated development runs.
- Use one shared disclosure-bar implementation, not separate approximations.

## Suggested priority order

### P0 — functionality and responsiveness

1. Diagnose and fix 20-second navigation and expensive tray restoration.
2. Make the Setups workflow capable of creating one valid setup end to end.
3. Fix invalid sentinel display (`32767 laps`) and investigate 0.050-second lap-time quantization.

### P1 — core product direction

1. Unify Live Telemetry around the richer Driving traces experience and make it customizable through the primary grid builder.
2. Redesign Race Analysis > Technical Data as an evidence-backed crew-chief/engineer workspace.
3. Stabilize live trace rendering and eliminate fuzzing/jitter.

### P2 — information architecture and consistency

1. Fix Race Analysis lap-row columns.
2. Make Home's rightmost metric consistent and meaningful; correct missing Best clean lap/Pace trend calculations or states.
3. Align Race Analysis and Progressive Tuning race-navigation workflows.
4. Replace generic Race Planning bottom-half advice with specific evidence-backed content or remove it.

### P3 — visual/layout cleanup

1. Remove excessive whitespace across Technical Data, Live Telemetry, and Settings.
2. Fix malformed glyphs and text wrapping.
3. Standardize Settings disclosure bars.
4. Correct popout window controls.
