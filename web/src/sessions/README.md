# Sessions library (`web/src/sessions/`)

Page: `pages/sessions.tsx`. Specification: `docs/08-rewrite/web-round-2-plan.md`, "Sessions" (backlog #17, #19, #18), "Round 3" (#33) and "Round 4" (#50-#57, #68-#72). Round 1's session tiles are gone; the list is one card per event, a 0.75 rem gap between cards (#50), the phases as lines inside it (#72).

| File | Holds |
|---|---|
| `model.ts` | Events and phases (running order P, Q, R), filters (`Selection`, `filterSessions`, `facetOptions`), the line rules (`phaseSlots`, `cellSlots`, `colouredFigures`), analysis exceptions, `eventPhaseOptions`. Pure; unit-tested. |
| `dates.ts` | Local date and time (audit S1). |
| `library.ts` | `useLibrary(customerId, library)`: the whole library in 200-row pages under one cache key per library (`main` keeps the plain URL); the filters work on it here. |
| `event-row.tsx` | `EventRow` (identity block and phase lines) and `PhaseLine`; `SetupLock` and `LockIcon`, the Fixed/Open lock glyph (#51, #69). |
| `phase-hues.test.ts` | The three phase hue tokens, shared with the investigation's P · Q · R selector (#52). |
| `silhouette.ts`, `track-silhouette.tsx` | Track silhouette from the analysis geometry, about 1.5 rem tall, fetched once per track layout when its row is on screen, kept in memory and in the device's storage. |
| `tab-memory.ts` | The last tab per session, remembered silently from the hash; the cell opens there. |
| `sessions.css` | All styling. Race data colours come from `--race-*`, phase hues from `--phase-practice-hue`, `--phase-qualify-hue`, `--phase-race-hue`, the car stripe from `--car-*`. |

## The card

- **One card per event** (#50): its own border and radius, a 0.75 rem gap to the next, no shared borders. The setup lock sits in the top-right corner of the identity block, inside it (#51, #69): closed for a fixed setup, open for an open one, the meaning on hover (`title`) and as the accessible name (`role="img"`, `aria-label`). A phase whose setup differs from its event carries its own lock beside its name.
- **Identity block and phase lines** (#72, which supersedes the side-by-side cells of #33 and #52): the identity at the left behind a divider, vertically centred against the lines; the phases are full-width lines stacked top to bottom in session order (P, Q, R; `groupEvents` sorts them). A missing phase has no line, so a race-only or practice-only event is one line and nothing is empty; the lines stay centred in a card the identity makes taller.
- **Identity** (#71; left, 15 to 18 rem, above the lines under 62 rem): the silhouette (1.5 rem) at the left, level with the track name's first line, then three lines on one left edge: the track (the only display-type line, one line where it fits, at most two), `config · date`, and the car on its own line behind its colour bar, which stands on that edge and spans the car's text. The block holds no space for a silhouette it does not have: while the outline is awaited its square is held so nothing moves when it arrives, and a layout known to have none draws and reserves nothing (`TrackSilhouette`). The series is in the car's tooltip, not drawn.
- **Phase line** (`PhaseLine`; `cellSlots` by `cellKind`): the head is the badge, the phase name and its time on one baseline (the time reads `1:00 PM · 2 REC`); the figures follow as labelled pairs, labels over figures, one gutter (1.25 rem) between columns and one spacing (0.625 rem) between rows, about 0.75 rem inside the panel. From 56 rem of line width the head is the first column (15 rem) and the figures sit in seven even columns on the head's baseline: the race in all seven, practice (Pos, Best, Long run, Fall-off) in columns 1, 3, 5, 7 and qualifying (Pos, Lap 1, Lap 2) in 1, 4, 7, so every column lines up from one phase to the next. Narrower, the head takes its own row and the figures wrap under it in even columns (the race four, three under 30 rem, two under 21 rem; practice four, two under 21 rem; qualifying three). The race line carries its hue strongest (a 5 px edge, a stronger tint) and its figures largest (18 px against the preamble's 14 px). Practice and qualifying are in the quiet classes `.sx-qv`/`.sx-qp` (14 px, secondary colour; the type audit counts them as secondary). Pos is `P3 / 24` (#55, `classification`): the official place over the field size (`results_position`, `field_size`, sent together from a later recording of the event), else the last recorded position alone, never a recorded place over an official field.
- **Race figures**: finish (`P4 ▲8`: the change against `start_position`, the official grid, #56; the grid position is not printed but is the arrow's hover and accessible name, `up 8 from P12`, #68), Grn · Cau, best, pace, stops, Burn (`fuel_per_lap_gal`, the backend's green burn per lap, `0.47 gal/lap`, #57), Long run (#54). A missing value is `—`. A phase with no figures shows its lap count and the one exception chip that explains it. The whole line is the link when the phase can open.
- **Layout by width** (container queries on the card and on each line, not a device test): the identity beside the lines at 62 rem of card width and wider, above them below that; each line's own width picks the row layout (56 rem and wider) or the wrapped one. Nothing sits behind a disclosure.
- **Type**: the owner's minimums (2026-10-03) beat the plan's 0.75 rem: names 16 px (`--text-body`), figures 15 px or more (`--text-data`; the race's 18 px, `--text-section-title`), labels and secondary lines 14 px (`--text-compact`). The card grows to fit; `perf/type-scale.mjs` selects `.sx-id h2`, `.sx-meta`, `.sx-car`, `.sx-l`, `.sx-v` and `.sx-p`, so the audit measures them. No local `--sx-*` tokens: the stylesheet reads the theme's tokens.

## Rules that are easy to get wrong

- **Colour budget.** Gain or loss on the position change (race only), the green-flag colour on a green-lap count above zero (#53), amber on a caution count above zero, the phase hue on the edge and badge, the car stripe. Nothing else: the finish is a plain number (no win flag), its arrow's hover names the grid position (#68), best, pace, stops, burn and long run carry no colour, there is no panel fill and no glow. The event's fastest lap is marked by weight (`data-event-best`), never by colour, and never across phases. `colouredFigures` is the tested budget: at most three per cell.
- **The finish change** is measured against `start_position` (the grid from the session results, else the backend's position at the green flag), never `starting_position` (the first recorded position). Without `start_position` there is no arrow and no hover reference; a held place has no arrow, and the slot's own hover (`Finished P1, started P1`) still names the start.
- **Pace slot.** The server's `green_pace_median_s` then the backend trend (`pace_slope_s_per_lap`) with its sign.
- **Chips.** Fixed/Open is a lock glyph, not a chip. Exception chips (`Not analyzed`, `Failed · reason`, `Deferred · reason`, `Queued`, `Analyzing`) appear only on a phase that is not ready; `Analyze` and `Retry` are the chip's own action. A ready phase shows nothing.
- **Filters** sit in the page heading by the shared width rule (`ui/filter-bar.tsx`): inline at the right when they fit, else `Filters · N` (popover on a pointer, sheet on touch). No library control: `lab` only by `?library=lab` in the URL (#34). Any chosen value within one filter, all filters together. The selection is saved as `session_filters` in the driver's prefs (the query values of `GET /sessions`: car_path, track name, series name, `race`/`qualify`/`practice`, `fixed`/`open`) and mirrored on the device only for the first paint. The Setup filter appears once a session states its setup type. Each dropdown button carries its own choice and clears from inside its list; no chips, no Clear in the heading (#35, #36).
- Fixed or Open comes from `is_fixed_setup` (true fixed, false open); null means the recording does not say: no lock, never read as open.
- The investigation header's P . Q . R selector takes the event's phases and their analysis state from the session detail (`event_sessions`), in the shell (`ui/investigation-slot.tsx`); its segments use the same phase hues (`PHASE_HUE` in `investigation/ui/PhaseSelector.tsx`).
- Round 1 figures kept: event best over the whole event whatever the filter shows (`groupEvents`), longest run and pace on every cell, the saved filter selections.
